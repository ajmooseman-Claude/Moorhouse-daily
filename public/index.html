Where do I find a calendar link?
  1. iCloud: on your iPhone open Calendar, tap Calendars, then the ⓘ beside a calendar. Turn on Public Calendar, tap Share Link, then Copy.
  2. Google: calendar.google.com › Settings › pick the calendar › Integrate calendar › Secret address in iCal format.
  3. Outlook / Microsoft 365: Outlook on the web › Settings › Calendar › Shared calendars › Publish a calendar, then copy the ICS link.

These links are read-only, but anyone who has one can see that calendar. They're stored on your own server, behind your password.

Adding a voice like Samantha

  1. Mac: System Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › System voice › Manage Voices, then tick Samantha under English (US).
  2. iPhone: Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices › English, then download Samantha.
  3. Reload this page and choose her under Reading voice at the top of this menu.
